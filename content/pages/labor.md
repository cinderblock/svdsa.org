<h2>Charter</h2>
<p><em>The Labor Working Group was officially chartered on 3/6/2018. It was previously chartered as a Lower Peninsula Branch WG on 11/27/2017.</em></p>
<p>As members of the Silicon Valley DSA, we propose the creation of a</p>
<ol class="lower-numeral">
<li>Labor Working Group</li>
<li>which would report to the Silicon Valley DSA Chapter</li>
<li>to fulfill the Following Objectives:
<ul>
<li>All workers in the Silicon Valley will be unionized with the eventual goal that all Silicon Valley companies will be controlled by workers.</li>
<li>All Silicon Valley workers will have living and equitable wages, benefits, and protections.</li>
<li>The demographics within each sector in tech will represent the demographics of the population at large.</li>
<li>All individuals will have a job or other means to live and thrive.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>using this initial strategy:</li>
<li><i>Objective #1: All workers in the Silicon Valley will be unionized with the eventual goal that all Silicon Valley companies will be controlled by workers.</i>
<ul>
<li>Building solidarity between blue collar and white collar workers in tech and across industries and unions.</li>
<li>Consciousness raising: encouraging tech workers to see themselves as workers and helping more people see unionization as an option.</li>
<li>Protecting workers who are trying to organize from reprisals through advocacy for policy protections and solidarity actions.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><i>Objective #2: All Silicon Valley workers will have living and equitable wages, benefits, and protections</i>
<ul>
<li>Advocating for legislative solutions and unionization in order to guarantee the following for all Silicon Valley workers:
<ul>
<li>Enforcement of the 40-hour work week for all salaried and hourly workers, with worker-driven, flexible, and predictable scheduling.</li>
<li>End of at will employment.</li>
<li>Guaranteed worker benefits, including:
<ul>
<li>Childcare for all</li>
<li>Paid parental leave for all</li>
<li>Living and equitable wages</li>
<li>Adequate time off</li>
<li>Mental and physical health protection in the workplace</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Advocating for a shorter work week.</li>
<li>Advocating for socially guaranteed benefits, rather than benefits that are tied to employment.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><i>Objective #3: The demographics within each sector in tech will represent the demographics of the population at large</i>
<ul>
<li>Demanding that pre-existing unions (e.g. AFL-CIO) be more attentive to broader social issues.<br />Protecting marginalized groups within tech.</li>
<li>Demanding that the hiring practices of tech companies are aimed at increasing diversity.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><i>Objective #4: All individuals will have a job or other means to live and thrive.</i>
<ul>
<li>Protecting worker rights in the face of increasing automation across industries.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>to last until above objectives are met.</li>
<li>We anticipate that we will need to spend no special allotment of funds other than for meeting space and incidental expenses such as printing.</li>
<li>Membership is open to all Silicon Valley DSA chapter members.</li>
</ol>
