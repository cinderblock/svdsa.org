The first step to receiving Silicon Valley DSA’s endorsement is to fill out our candidate questionnaire.

Candidates for city council and county supervisor races should fill out the questionnaire found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19pPaWAyTy9KcyIoqTIpDWYfl7Gmg5p1KcvmwTHpDjRg/edit).

Candidates for local school boards and county board of education races should fill out the questionnaire found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/171ssTX8iFn-wOsQ0WSmKUah997CSzPhT8loEpavh3KU/edit).

Please email a copy of your filled questionnaire to [electoral@siliconvalleydsa.org](mailto:electoral@siliconvalleydsa.org)
