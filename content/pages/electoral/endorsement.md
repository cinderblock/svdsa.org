<p>The first step to receiving Silicon Valley DSA&#8217;s endorsement is to fill out our candidate questionnaire.</p>
<p>Candidates for city council and county supervisor races should fill out the questionnaire found <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/19pPaWAyTy9KcyIoqTIpDWYfl7Gmg5p1KcvmwTHpDjRg/edit">here</a>.</p>
<p>Candidates for local school boards and county board of education races should fill out the questionnaire found <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/171ssTX8iFn-wOsQ0WSmKUah997CSzPhT8loEpavh3KU/edit">here</a>.</p>
<p>Please email a copy of your filled questionnaire to <a href="mailto:electoral@siliconvalleydsa.org">electoral@siliconvalleydsa.org</a></p>
