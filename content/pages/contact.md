## Grievances

SV DSA is committed to providing a safe and inclusive space in our Chapter for comrades of all genders, races, classes, and backgrounds. If you have ever felt uncomfortable because of something an SVDSA Member has said or done, even if you're not sure that it constitutes "harassment", reach out to our elected Harassment Grievance Officers by filling out **[this Grievance Report Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1YGZIftsaZGCvtPY2HN4sVb91CakMTiIfVtLKiZfiOo0/viewform)**. For more details, check out our [Harassment Grievance Policy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aVKRB29mZs2B-aGfaVKIcurZ00PmurY9W-pyyXn54gY/edit?usp=sharing).
