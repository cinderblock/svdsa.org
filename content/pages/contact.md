<h2>Grievances</h2>
<p>SV DSA is committed to providing a safe and inclusive space in our Chapter for comrades of all genders, races, classes, and backgrounds.</p>
<p>If you have ever felt uncomfortable because of something an SVDSA Member has said or done, even if you&#8217;re not sure that it constitutes &#8220;harassment&#8221;, reach out to our elected Harassment Grievance Officers by filling out <strong><a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1YGZIftsaZGCvtPY2HN4sVb91CakMTiIfVtLKiZfiOo0/viewform" target="_blank">this Grievance Report Form</a></strong>.</p>
<p>For more details, check out our <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aVKRB29mZs2B-aGfaVKIcurZ00PmurY9W-pyyXn54gY/edit?usp=sharing" rel="noopener">Harassment Grievance Policy</a>.</p>
