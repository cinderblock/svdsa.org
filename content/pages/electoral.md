<h2>Charter</h2>
<p><em>The Electoral Working Group was officially chartered on 8/30/2018.</em></p>
<p>As members of Silicon Valley DSA, we propose the creation of an</p>
<ol class="lower-numeral">
<li>Electoral Working Group</li>
<li>Which would report to the Silicon Valley DSA chapter</li>
<li>Which would have the following objectives:
<ul>
<li>Provide a thorough database of information on elected officials and their policy positions for use by the chapter.</li>
<li>Elect socialists to representative offices.</li>
<li>Pass ballot initiatives which directly address the material needs of the working class, and which strengthen worker power.</li>
<li>Provide the public with reliable informational materials on elections from a democratic socialist perspective.</li>
<li>Increase voter turnout in elections, including amongst historically disenfranchised communities</li>
<li>Advocate for initiatives and reforms that create a more equitable electoral system.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Using these initial strategies:
<ul>
<li>Research and Endorsements:
<ul>
<li>Research currently elected official’s policy positions and voting history for use by the chapter.</li>
<li>Create a recurring city-council watch, which will attend local city council meetings and keep the chapter appraised of the actions of local city governments.</li>
<li>Research and provide recommendations to the chapter on candidates and initiatives seeking our endorsement.</li>
<li>Research and evaluate candidates and initiatives not immediately seeking our endorsement, but which may be of interest to the chapter, and provide a recommendation on their endorsement.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Campaigning and Elections:
<ul>
<li>Coordinate campaigning and outreach for endorsed ballot initiatives and candidates with other working groups and the chapter as a whole at the discretion of the members of the working group.</li>
<li>Create voting information materials for each election cycle, the formatting and distribution of which should be coordinated with the Socialist Media Committee.</li>
<li>Create a voter guide to be approved by the chapter before each election.</li>
<li>Manage and implement the candidate questionnaire and endorsement process.</li>
<li>Work with other working groups whenever possible to handle campaigns.</li>
<li>Coordinate voter registration efforts and drives to get individuals to vote.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Duration:
<ul>
<li>Until the objectives are accomplished, or the working group feels that it has the momentum and resources to broaden their objectives or initial strategies.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Anticipated Use of Funds:
<ul>
<li>We anticipate that we will need to spend no special allotment of funds other than for meeting space and incidental expenses such as printing.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Membership:
<ul>
<li>Would be open to members of Silicon Valley DSA.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
