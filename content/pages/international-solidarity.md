<h2>Charter</h2>
<p><em>The International Solidarity working group was officially chartered on 02/24/2024.</em></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Silicon Valley DSA has a record of standing in solidarity with the Palestinian people. In 2021, as Israel expelled Palestinian families in Jerusalem’s Sheikh Jarrah and Silwan neighborhoods to make room for settlers, we condemned Israel&#8217;s violence, reiterated our support for the anti-racist Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions (BDS) campaign, and recognized the long-standing impacts of Zionist ethnic cleansing from the 1948 Nakba (</span><a href="https://siliconvalleydsa.org/2021/05/15/palestine/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nakba Day 2021 statement</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">).</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We believe Palestinians have a right to resist apartheid and occupation. Every attempt to self-determine and pursue this liberation peacefully, such as the Great March of Return of 2018-19, has been met with violent repression. Palestinians in the Occupied Palestinian Territories have long suffered daily abuse, harassment, torture, and undeserved detainment at the hands of Israel&#8217;s Occupation Forces (IOF). The settler-colonial state of Israel made peaceful change impossible, and this led directly to the events of October 7 (</span><a href="https://www.dsausa.org/statements/end-the-violence-end-the-occupation-free-palestine/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">DSA National statement</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">). Israel&#8217;s genocidal siege and collective punishment of Gaza since then has made clear to the world the depravity of Israel that Palestinians have long desperately cried out against.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Just as Zionism has harmed Palestinians through the establishment of a Jewish supremacist ethnostate, so too has the Hindutva ideology of Hindu supremacy harmed the minority Muslim population of India and caste-oppressed groups. We supported SB 403, CA&#8217;s anti-caste-discrimination bill, in the face of immense opposition and lobbying from U.S. Hindu supremacist groups such as Vishva Hindu Parishad of America (</span><a href="https://siliconvalleydsa.org/2023/07/20/chapter-committee-statement-on-assemblymember-alex-lee-and-sb403/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">statement</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">).</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Silicon Valley DSA set up a provisional Palestine Solidarity group in October in response to the ongoing genocide of Gaza. Since then, we have helped organize local ceasefire resolutions in San Jose (which issued a </span><a href="https://www.instagram.com/omartorresd3/p/C2NlnOtvD9M/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">statement</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">), </span><a href="https://sccdp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/A-Call-for-Immediate-Cease-fire-in-Gaza-and-Provision-of-Humanitarian-Aid.pdf"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Santa Clara County&#8217;s Democratic Party</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Redwood City, and Santa Clara; participated in No Money for Massacres phonebanks and held our own &#8220;fax banks&#8221; to Senators; turned out members to protests; dropped banners over freeways, handed out fliers, and held a teach-in in Redwood City; and worked in coalition to successfully pressure Reps Ro Khanna and Zoe Lofgren to support a ceasefire.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Objectives</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The proposed International Solidarity Working Group seeks to build upon our past work, and provide both resources and a local socialist organizing home for those who seek to stand in solidarity with the Palestinian people; against ethno-supremacist ideologies such as Hindutva and caste oppression; and against the U.S. military-industrial complex, imperialism, colonization, and oppression more broadly everywhere.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Our initial objectives for this Working Group through to the 2025 Chapter Convention are to:</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Organize locally and nationally to achieve a ceasefire in Palestine</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Support the BDS campaign, including by organizing </span><a href="https://bdsmovement.net/apartheid-free-zones"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Apartheid Free Zones</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, banning state-sponsored trips to Israel, and ending City/County investments in BDS targets.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Organize for reparations to the Palestinian people, and all those who have been similarly wronged by U.S.-sponsored genocide, colonialism, and imperialism.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Build coalition with local anti-imperialist organizations such as San Jose Against War (SJAW), Palestinian Youth Movement (PYM), Arab Resource and Organizing Center (AROC), Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR) SF Bay Area, Jewish Voice for Peace (JVP) South Bay, and the San Jose Peace and Justice Center (SJPJC).</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Support the efforts of DSA National&#8217;s International Committee, such as No Money for Massacres, as well as global anti-imperialist and anti-capitalist campaigns by our international affiliate Progressive International. We will also work in solidarity with national groups such as the US Campaign for Palestinian Rights (USCPR) and CODEPINK.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Identify candidates for elected office who will work through a socialist lens to dismantle the U.S. military-industrial complex, and submit them to the Chapter for endorsement consideration.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Educate and mobilize new and existing members on Palestine solidarity and the broader anti-imperialist and anti-colonialist struggle</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Grow capacity and develop leaders for internationalist organizing in Silicon Valley DSA</span></li>
</ol>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Initial Strategy</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Our actions to achieve our Objectives, until the 2025 Convention, will include but not be limited to:</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local organizing, including legislation</span>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will continue to support efforts to pass a ceasefire resolution in Redwood City, as well as explore new efforts in San Mateo County and Santa Clara County.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will support SJAW&#8217;s efforts to end City-authorized travel to Israel, as well as similar BDS campaigns to divest from targets. We will also explore Apartheid-Free Zones more broadly, outside of elected bodies.</span></li>
</ol>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Direct actions. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">As many of these are time-sensitive, we request as part of this Charter&#8217;s approval that Silicon Valley DSA&#8217;s organizational endorsement and logo may be used to endorse individual rallies, protests, calls to actions, press releases, or other such materials in connection with International Solidarity and Palestine Solidarity efforts, subject to approval by a majority of the Chapter Committee, without requiring membership approval at monthly Chapter meetings</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will organize or support vigils, pickets, and protests to achieve a ceasefire and end the genocide in Palestine.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will turn out our Chapter members to nearby protests and other direct actions that we endorse.</span></li>
</ol>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">National campaigns</span>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will support the DSA International Committee&#8217;s efforts to end the genocide in Gaza, including through phone banks to DSA members nationally to hold Congress and the President accountable.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will remain connected with USCPR on effective local organizing efforts. USCPR has supported local ceasefire campaigns and is now shifting to divestment campaigns.</span></li>
</ol>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Education</span>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will host events such as teach-ins, and support art, fliers, and other propaganda, to educate the general public about Palestine, imperialism, and colonialism.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will continue to publicize our actions through social media, flyering, and more, with the goal to bring in more new members to the socialist, anti-imperialist struggle.</span></li>
</ol>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Coalition Building</span>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will continue to support the organizing efforts of SJAW, PYM, AROC, CAIR SF Bay Area, JVP South Bay, SJPJC, and other similar organizations that stand against imperialist warmongering.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We will strengthen connections with militant labor unions, local community groups, and others who align with us </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">against all war but class war</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Duration</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This Working Group will exist until all objectives are met, and as re-chartered annually at Convention per Silicon Valley DSA&#8217;s bylaws.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anticipated Use of Funds</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We anticipate that we will need to spend no special allotment of funds other than for meeting spaces and incidental expenses such as printing and supplies. This amount should not exceed $500 until the 2025 Convention. Beyond this amount, we will fundraise for expenses as needed.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Membership Criteria</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Membership is open to all Silicon Valley DSA chapter members.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Leadership</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This Working Group shall have up to 3 co-chairs, who shall serve 1-year terms through to the next Chapter Convention. Pursuant to Article VII, Section 6 of our </span><a href="https://siliconvalleydsa.org/bylaws/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">bylaws</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, at least 1 co-chair position will be reserved for a non-male applicant. At least 1 co-chair position will also be reserved for each of our branches (San Jose and Lower Peninsula), if there are applicants from each branch.</span></p>
