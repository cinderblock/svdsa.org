## What We Do

The Membership Committee helps new members smoothly navigate our chapter and get plugged in to our work. We also regularly develop our existing members into organizers and leaders. If you want to help with welcoming new Chapter members and supporting current members, please join us!

## Upcoming Events

The Membership Committee does not meet on a regular basis. Reach out to an Officer if you’re interested in joining.
