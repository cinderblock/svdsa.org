<p><i>This document collects ballot endorsements made by Silicon Valley DSA for the 2018 general elections and related signature-gathering efforts. Updated: October 24, 2018</i></p>
<h2><b><i>Summary:</i></b></h2>
<ul>
<li>In California:
<ul>
<li>For housing justice, vote <b>yes on</b> proposition<b> 10,</b> which allows cities to implement stronger rent control policies, and propositions <b>1 &amp; 2</b>, which are both bond measures that finance construction of affordable housing.</li>
<li>For healthcare justice, vote <b>yes on</b> proposition <b>8</b>, which prevents dialysis clinics from price-gouging on life saving medical care.</li>
<li>Vote <b>yes on</b> proposition <b>12 </b>to prevent cruelty to farm animals.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>In Local Races:
<ul>
<li>In Palo Alto, vote <b>yes on</b> proposition <b>Proposition F</b> to prevent Stanford from price-gouging on healthcare and the expense of working conditions for healthcare workers and preventing patient infections.</li>
<li>In East Palo Alto, vote for <b>Patricia Lopez </b>for city council.</li>
<li>In Redwood City, vote for <b>Diana Reddy </b>for city council.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2><b><i>State Ballot Endorsements &#8211; Vote Yes!</i></b></h2>
<p>Official info provided by the state of California: <a href="http://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/">http://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/</a> Includes title, summary, analysis, and full text.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Proposition 1 &#8211; Authorizes Bonds to Fund Specified Housing Assistance Programs</strong>
<ul>
<li>Authorizes $4 billion of state general obligation bonds to fund existing housing programs.</li>
<li>Includes $1.5 billion for Multifamily Housing for low-income residents, $1 billion for loans to help veterans to purchase farms and homes.</li>
<li>Includes $450 million for infill and transit-oriented housing projects, $300 million for farmworker housing, and $300 million for manufactured and mobile homes.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Proposition 2 &#8211; Authorizes Bonds to Fund Existing Housing Programs for Individuals with Mental Illness</strong>
<ul>
<li>Ratifies existing law establishing the No Place Like Home Program, which finances permanent housing for individuals with mental illness who are homeless or at risk for chronic homelessness.</li>
<li>Ratifies issuance of up to $2 billion prev. authorized bonds to finance No Place Like Home Program.</li>
<li>Amends Mental Health Services Act to authorize transfers of up to $140 million a year of Mental Health Services Fund to No Place Like Home Program.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Proposition 8 &#8211; Regulates Amounts Outpatient Kidney Dialysis Clinics Charge for Dialysis Treatment</strong>
<ul>
<li>Dialysis clinics routinely overcharge patients, in some cases, more than three times the cost of providing care. Prop 8 would end price gouging by:
<ul>
<li>Limiting charges to 15% greater than the total cost of direct patient care and quality improvement costs.</li>
<li>Prohibiting clinics from refusing to treat patient based on the source of payment for care.</li>
<li>Requiring annual reporting to the state regarding clinic costs, patient charges, and revenue; with rebates and penalties if charges exceed the limit.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Proposition 10 &#8211; Expands Local Governments’ Authority to Enact Rent Control on Residential Property</strong>
<ul>
<li>Vote yes on the Affordable Housing Act, as all renters deserve to benefit from the protection of rent control.</li>
<li>Would repeal the current state law that severely restricts the scope of existing rent control policies, allowing cities to provide rent control in newer apartments, duplexes, and single family homes.</li>
<li>Also Allows policies that would limit the rental rates that residential-property owners may charge for new tenants, which would remove their incentive to evict existing tenants.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Proposition 12 &#8211; Establishes New Standards for Confinement of Specified Farm Animals; Bans Sale of Noncomplying Products</strong>
<ul>
<li>Creates new standards for confinement of some farm animals, including chickens and pigs, and adds enforcement to existing law. Reduces suffering, improves food safety, and protects the environment.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
