Our Labor Working Group comrades are encouraging residents of [State Senate District 13](https://sd13.senate.ca.gov/district) to write to Senator Josh Becker and ask him to speak out in solidarity with striking UC workers. Feel free to use the script below or write your own message and send to [alex.kobayashi@sen.ca.gov](mailto:alex.kobayashi@sen.ca.gov) and [senator.becker@senate.ca.gov.](mailto:senator.becker@senate.ca.gov)

Script:

*Hi Senator Becker,*  
*I am writing as a constituent concerned with labor issues in your district. I know you have been a strong supporter of labor in the past as demonstrated by your facing arrest for joining workers on the picket line at SFO (1). As I am sure you are aware, over 48,000 academic workers employed across the state at the UC system have gone on strike for better working conditions.*

*Since you are a Founding Trustee of UC Merced, I wanted to ask you to release a public statement in solidarity with the striking workers. Your position as my elected official, your past support for labor, and your status as a Founding Trustee at a UC campus uniquely position you to have an impact in support of academic workers looking to have the dignity of supporting themselves and their families through labor.*  
*Sincerely,*  
*\[NAME\]*

1.  [https://www.google.com/amp/s/www.foxbusiness.com/economy/san-francisco-airport-workers-arrested-cited-civil-disobedience.amp](https://www.google.com/amp/s/www.foxbusiness.com/economy/san-francisco-airport-workers-arrested-cited-civil-disobedience.amp)
