If you would like to donate monthly to the Mutual Aid fund, this is the page where you can do so. Thank you so much for considering!

The Mutual Aid Fund Tracker with current balances and amount spent, along with other details about the Mutual Aid Fund, can be found on our [Donation Page.](https://siliconvalleydsa.org/donate/)

If you would like to donate one-time instead, please use this link and select the “Mutual Aid Fund” under the drop-down options:

**[One-Time Donation](https://siliconvalleydsa.org/donate)**

If you would like to donate monthly to the SV DSA general fund instead, please use this link:

**[Monthly Local Dues](https://siliconvalleydsa.org/join/#local_dues)**
