<p><span style="font-size: x-large;"><b>Today, a nuclear-armed state</b></span> rains down terror on an oppressed population of natives, behind a multi-billion dollar shield of rockets protecting its settlers. Built on the ethnic cleansing of almost a million natives, the occupying state imposes a system of <a href="https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/mde15/5141/2022/en/">apartheid</a> and <a href="https://togetheragainstapartheid.org/apartheid-laws-in-israel/">ethnosupremacy</a> over its claimed territories, placing its own settlers&#8217; rights above the natives&#8217; democratic will. Despite the natives having an <a href="https://law4palestine.org/do-palestinians-have-the-right-to-resist-and-what-are-the-limits-short-article/">internationally recognized right to resist</a> their subjugation and pursue self-determination, other major powers refuse to recognize this – saying only the occupier has a &#8220;right to self defense.&#8221;</p>
<p>We could have written a similar introduction about any number of colonial and occupied groups. White Americans and Indigenous people – who were similarly genocided under the guise of &#8220;manifest destiny.&#8221; <a href="https://www.uchicagounited.org/diso-2024/hindutva-solidarity-for-kashmir">Hindutva supremacists</a> and occupied Kashmir – where Kashmiris have been denied their internationally recognized right to a plebiscite and self-determination.</p>
<p>As Israel today continues its genocide on Palestine and begins to destroy Lebanon, we must be clear as socialists that we oppose not only Israel&#8217;s actions, but also the racist, colonial ideology underpinning it all. Just as DSA has previously taken stances on anti-racism, anti-colonialism, and anti-imperialism,<b> today we commit to organizing around dismantling Zionism in our local governments and communities</b>.</p>
<p><b>At our end-August chapter meeting, Silicon Valley DSA </b><a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1otufYpHJ3dvuu81M5jlNWixaM-zvO6Wz4Gf5MTpcGkk/edit?usp=sharing"><b>unanimously passed a resolution</b></a><b> reinforcing our chapter as an anti-Zionist organization in principle and practice.</b></p>
<p>Zionism, as defined by Theodor Herzl in the late 19th century, has always been a settler-colonial project intended to dispossess natives – to create “a wall of Europe against Asia… <a href="https://www.palestinechronicle.com/herzls-vision-realized-israel-as-outpost-of-western-civilisation-in-asia/">an outpost of [Western] civilization against [Eastern] barbarism</a>.&#8221; Armed with the lie of &#8220;<a href="https://decolonizepalestine.com/myth/a-land-without-a-people-for-a-people-without-a-land/">a land without a people for a people without a land</a>,&#8221; Zionists regularly terrorized Palestinians alongside the British, and built <a href="https://jacobin.com/2016/10/kibbutz-labor-zionism-bernie-sanders-ben-gurion/">their own segregated systems</a> during the years of the British Mandate. During the <i>Nakba</i> (&#8220;catastrophe&#8221;) of 1948, Zionists prevailed in ethnically cleansing 750,000 Palestinians to form the state of Israel – against the consent of most natives.</p>
<p>In opposing Zionism, SVDSA seeks to end all forms of oppression and ethnosupremacy, in line with our prior opposition to caste discrimination and white supremacy.</p>
<p>Since October 2023, SVDSA has mobilized to call for a ceasefire, helped pass ceasefire resolutions at the Democratic Party and local levels, initiated Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions (BDS) campaigns, and educated our neighbors about Israel&#8217;s genocide. We recognize that, as U.S. taxpayers, we are directly paying for the destruction of Palestine – to the tune of $3.8 billion in annual military funding, and a further $10+ billion in arms approved just this year. Redirecting these funds alone would give <b>$1250 per year for every American child in poverty</b>.</p>
<p>Following the lead of other DSA chapters, including DSA SF and East Bay DSA, we drafted a resolution to fully commit our chapter to anti-Zionism, and place ourselves firmly on the side of the struggle for Palestinian liberation.</p>
<p>The adopted resolution clearly defines anti-Zionist expectations for our membership and endorsed candidates. With the passage of this resolution, Zionist positions — such as opposing BDS or the Palestinian right of return — are considered to be in substantial disagreement with Silicon Valley DSA’s principles and policies. Supporting Zionist lobby groups — such as Christians United for Israel (CUFI), the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC), and Democratic Majority for Israel (DMFI) — is also now a violation of our principles, because we recognize how these lobbies disingenuously provide political cover for Israel&#8217;s genocide. Members who commit these violations can be subject to expulsion, as is the case with violating any other DSA principles.</p>
<p>Along the same lines, our endorsed candidates must now publicly support BDS, disaffiliate from any Zionist lobby groups, and — when elected — politically support the Palestinian cause and oppose Zionist legislation.</p>
<p>Our resolution against Zionism is a product of our diverse chapter. Just as we firmly rejected any conflation between Hinduism and ethno-nationalist Hindutva in opposing caste discrimination in 2023, so too do we reject any conflation of Judaism and Zionism. We recognize and commend the long history of Jewish anti-Zionist and non-Zionist organizations – such as Jewish Voice for Peace, IfNotNow, the 20th-century socialist <a href="https://jacobin.com/2024/05/antisemitism-bill-bund-jewish-history">Jewish Labour Bund</a>, and the modern <a href="https://www.derbund.org/">Jewish Labor Bund</a> – as well as the efforts of our own anti-Zionist and non-Zionist Jewish members.</p>
<p>We expect our anti-Zionist resolution will make us an even stronger ally in the struggle for a free Palestine, and commend the work of several local organizations and coalitions fighting towards this end, including <a href="https://www.instagram.com/bayareapym/">Palestinian Youth Movement</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/aroc_bayarea/">Arab Resource &amp; Organizing Center</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/jvpbayarea/">Jewish Voice for Peace</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/ca15_for_palestine/">CA15forPalestine</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/vigil4gaza/">Vigil4Gaza</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/sit_in_to_stop_genocide/">Stanford Against Apartheid in Palestine</a>, and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/sanjoseagainstwar">San Jose Against War</a>. We look forward to working more closely with our allies, who have made it clear that DSA nationally must explicitly connect the fight against Zionism with our socialist and anti-colonialist principles.</p>
<p><b>We commit to making Palestine central in our ongoing struggle against global capitalism, settler colonialism, and U.S. imperialism. Palestine will be free, from the river to the sea!</b></p>
