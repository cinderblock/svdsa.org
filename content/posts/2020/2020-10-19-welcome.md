Welcome to the blog for the Silicon Valley Democratic Socialists of America! As a part of our ongoing fight for working class power, we recognize that knowledge is key to organizing. Here you’ll find content like:

*   Official statements and press releases from SV DSA
*   Updates on relevant events, projects, and causes
*   Personal reflections from our members
*   Our weekly newsletter (Not on our list yet? [Sign up today](https://siliconvalleydsa.org/join/)!)

Read accounts from others in our community, hot takes on local issues, and meaningful calls to action. We encourage you to share these articles on social media and beyond, and make sure others in your community stay up to date on the fight for freedom, equality, and economic justice.
