Join Silicon Valley DSA for our October General Meeting! This meeting will feature updates from our working groups and ongoing projects, as well as a space to connect with comrades and discuss our current and future work as a Chapter. We will also have a skill-building session on "How to Crack Public Speaking"! Towards the end at 3-4 PM, we will phone bank our members whose DSA membership is expiring about renewing, Measure A, and Prop 50.

We welcome everyone to our meetings, whether you've been involved for a long time or are curious to learn more. To take part in Chapter decisions as a voting member, please sign up with National DSA at <a href="https://dsausa.org/join">https://dsausa.org/join</a>.

This meeting will be held in person, with the option to participate virtually via Zoom.
