Come join the International Solidarity Working Group and participate in our first No Appetite for Apartheid canvass in Redwood City!

No Appetite for Apartheid is a national campaign that follows the principles of the Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions (BDS) movement to reduce US support for Israeli apartheid and genocide against Palestinians. The campaign urges local small businesses to stop carrying Israeli food products and educates community members about supporting Palestine by boycotting Israeli products, a time-tested consumer tactic used against states perpetrating injustice. During our canvass, we’ll be visiting businesses in Redwood City and asking them to drop Israeli food products as well as to put up a flyer showing the community their support for Palestinians.
