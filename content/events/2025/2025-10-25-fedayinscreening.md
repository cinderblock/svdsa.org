<p class="_ap3a _aaco _aacu _aacx _aad7 _aade" dir="auto">Join Stanford SJP for a documentary on Georges Abdallah, the longest-detained political prisoner in Europe, and the links between Palestinian-Lebanese liberation and carceral struggles here and abroad.</p>
<p>Across movements, activists have rallied to support political prisoners and demand their release. Come watch, reflect, and connect the dots between international solidarity and decarceral organizing on campus.</p>
<p>📽️ Screening: October 25th, 4-6:30pm<br />
📍 Location: Oshman Hall</p>
<p>Stay for a post-screening panel on the connections between national liberation, decarceral struggle, and internationalism, followed by dinner and a mixer where you can chat with activists/organizers and get involved.</p>
<p>Liberté pour les prisonniers politiques. Solidarité internationale. ✊🇵🇸🇱🇧</p>
