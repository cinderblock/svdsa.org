On August 21, we are mobilizing for a public meeting calling on the Santa Clara County Housing, Land Use Environmental and Transportation (HLUET) committee to **divest** from companies complicit in genocide! Show up at 10 AM at 70 W Hedding St, San Jose or [on Zoom](https://plandev.santaclaracounty.gov/hearings-and-committees/housing-land-use-environment-and-transportation-committee), to give public comment for items not on the agenda and speak out in support of divesting from companies complicit in genocide!

If you’re interested in attending this event and want to connect with the organizers, [join our Google Group here](https://groups.google.com/g/sv-dsa-wg-international/about) to get in contact with the organizers! Click “Ask to join group” if you have a Gmail account, or “Contact owners and managers” if you do not. You can also reach out to [#wg-international-solidarity](https://siliconvalleydsa.slack.com/archives/C063N6CT475) on our Slack.
