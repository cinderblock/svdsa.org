Join Stanford SJP for a documentary on Georges Abdallah, the longest-detained political prisoner in Europe, and the links between Palestinian-Lebanese liberation and carceral struggles here and abroad. Across movements, activists have rallied to support political prisoners and demand their release. Come watch, reflect, and connect the dots between international solidarity and decarceral organizing on campus. 📽️ Screening: October 25th, 4-6:30pm 📍 Location: Oshman Hall Stay for a post-screening panel on the connections between national liberation, decarceral struggle, and internationalism, followed by dinner and a mixer where you can chat with activists/organizers and get involved.
