<p>If you want to build labor power, now is the time. The Starbucks Workers are on the brink of walk outs and pickets.  The Labor Working Group will be planning how we support them.</p>
<p>We are building up capacity, becoming familiar with (and trained in) Emergency Workplace Organizing Committee activities. EWOC is a mutual effort by DSA (nationally) and the UE (United Electrical) Union. Do you want to organize your workplace? Do you want to learn more about how? Join the Labor WG!</p>
<p>Efforts are underway to create a strong DSA presence in the South Bay Labor Council, and to be more closely connected to unions in the South Bay.</p>
<p>Please join us on zoom, Thursday, November 6th, from 6:30-7:30.</p>
