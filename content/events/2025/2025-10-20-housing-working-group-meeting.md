Hello, comrades! Please join the Housing Working Group for its next meeting, where we’ll elect co-chairs and discuss campaigns to organize for affordable housing, tenants’ rights, and an end to homelessness.

Please register for the Zoom meeting here: [https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/0pk5iWHkRUCRW1KCKL0Ivw](https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/0pk5iWHkRUCRW1KCKL0Ivw)

You can join our Google Group to keep up to date via email. Please visit [https://groups.google.com/g/sv-dsa-wg-housing-development/about](https://groups.google.com/g/sv-dsa-wg-housing-development/about) and click either “Ask to join group” or “Contact owners and managers”

**Everyone is encouraged to run for co-chair!** The main responsibility of co-chairs is to set up the meetings on Zoom and their agendas.
