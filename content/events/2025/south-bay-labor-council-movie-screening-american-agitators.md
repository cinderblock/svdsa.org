<p>The Labor WG of Silicon Valley DSA invites all labor-interested folks to join us in attending the screening of &#8220;American Agitators,&#8221; which won the Best Documentary award at Cinequest in San Jose this year! In the words of one review, it &#8220;chronicles the life and teachings of Fred Ross, Sr., who understood that inequality leads to injustice. Inspiring a new generation of activists to organize for systemic change, the film shows how collective action can combat racism, bigotry, and greed.&#8221;</p>
<p>Free admission.</p>
<p>Come join your Labor WG comrades in solidarity with the South Bay Labor Council, as we build a stronger relationship between our chapter and organized labor.</p>
<p>And stay tuned for a later screening, by the Labor Working Group, of &#8220;Union,&#8221; the documentary of the fight for the Amazon Labor Union. That will happen late in September, or early October.</p>
<p>Hope to see you on Friday the 5th of September! Help spread the word.</p>
