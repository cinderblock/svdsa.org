<p>In November, we advocated to the San Jose Code Enforcement Appeals Board with the tenants at Summerwind Apartments in East San Jose, who have been in a battle against their landlords due to their poor living conditions, including issues with pests and mold.</p>
<p>At the November meeting, code enforcement promised action &#8211; and updates &#8211; but no change occurred for these residents. So, we&#8217;re returning to hold code enforcement to their word.</p>
<p>Join us on Thursday, December 11 at 6:30 pm to support the Summerwind Tenant Union and call for action for the landlords&#8217; code violations. The hearing will take place in the Council Chambers at San Jose City Hall (200 E. Santa Clara Street). Be there and help us show up for tenants&#8217; rights!</p>
<p><strong>Location details:</strong><br />
San  Jose City Hall<br />
200 E Santa Clara St.<br />
San Jose, CA 95113</p>
<p><strong>Meeting location:<br />
</strong>We will gather outside of chambers. The meeting will be held in council chambers, but not through the traditional entrance for council meetings. The entrance will be on the first/ground floor at the back of the rotunda (south side, think of Santa Clara Street being the &#8220;front.&#8221;) Follow the hallway and a placard will indicate council chambers. A map exists below with arrows indicating entrance.</p>
