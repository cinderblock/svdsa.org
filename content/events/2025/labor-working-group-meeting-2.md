Let’s use the energies of the Labor Day weekend to grow SV DSA’s presence and strength in labor movements in the South Bay!

Much to report on, and to plan: supporting up-coming organizing efforts and strike planning. Specifically, stepping up to volunteer as Solidarity Captains for the Starbucks workers fight, and for tech worker organizing; creating liaisons with EWOC, the Emergency Workplace Organizing Committee; creating a sub-group to organize current DSA union members to work in a variety of ways to strengthen our power.

And a movie night (following up on “American Agitators”) to plan and begin outreach for. A movie that will touch on an ongoing struggle to unionize Amazon!
