<p>Let&#8217;s use the energies of the Labor Day weekend to grow SV DSA&#8217;s presence and strength in labor movements in the South Bay!</p>

<p>And a movie night (following up on &#8220;American Agitators&#8221;) to plan and begin outreach for. A movie that will touch on an ongoing struggle to unionize Amazon!</p>
