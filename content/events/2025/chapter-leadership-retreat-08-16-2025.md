<p>At our April Chapter Convention, we passed a resolution to hold chapter leadership retreats quarterly. These are events where we strategize about long-term plans, set mile markers, and generally do the work that keeps the chapter running strongly. We will be meeting on Saturday, August 16 at SEIU 521 Union Hall from 9-1 for a retreat. All members of chapter leadership are invited &#8211; steering committee, working group co-chairs, and active members looking to get more deeply involved in the nuts-and-bolts of running the chapter. This will be an in-person event with no Zoom option.</p>
