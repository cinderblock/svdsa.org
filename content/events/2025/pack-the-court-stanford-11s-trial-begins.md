<p>[URGENT ACTION] ‼️PACK THE COURT‼️ for the Stanford 11 at 8:30 AM on Monday November 17 at the Hall of Justice, 190 W Hedding Street, San Jose, CA</p>
<p>In June 2024, students, alumni, and one student reporter were arrested at a sit-in of the office of Stanford University’s president in protest of Stanford’s complicity with Israel’s genocide in Palestine. In Santa Clara County, the District Attorney Jeff Rosen has chosen to charge these protesters against genocide with felonies, and 11 Stanford community members have been indicted on two grand jury charges, making them the first out of thousands of protesters across the nation in the past two years to go to felony trial over their pro-Palestinian activism.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.instagram.com/sit_in_to_stop_genocide/p/DRAwnkVksNZ/">The next court date for the Stanford 11 is November 17</a>, where trial proceedings are set to be scheduled and assigned. Show up and pack the court in order to support the Stanford 11, as they fight trumped up charges just for their courage to call out Stanford&#8217;s complicity in the genocide!</p>
