Please join our public facing education event, hosted by the Political Education working group, which will be on understanding our local government.

Please note that these events are <em>typically</em> hosted on the 4th Wednesday of each month, but will be one week later this time to account for the Measure A mobilization. Topics will be announced monthly and resources will be published online as well as through our newsletter. These meetings are a great way to learn the why behind the work we do.
