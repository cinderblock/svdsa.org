<p>Curious about socialism but don’t know where to start? In need of some comradely time to unwind? Want to connect with other local comrades and build community? Join us for our October Last Friday Social!</p>
<p>This month, we will be taking a 90 minute guided tour to learn about the history of activism and progressivism in San José. It begins at the San José Peace &amp; Justice Center and ends near city hall.</p>
<p>After the tour, we will meet nearby to socialize, eat, and drink at Peanuts Deluxe Cafe, a business that recently changed ownership and became a co-op. Peanuts will also be hosting open mic night, so feel free to prepare something if you want to participate!</p>
<p>Hope you can make it! Feel free to bring friends, family, or anyone else who is interested.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
