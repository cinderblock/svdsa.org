Join the SVDSA in a bench-building session hosted by the transit working group. In collaboration with SFBABC, we're building benches to be installed at bench-less bus stops to help provide relief to those who rely on transit, and to continue making transit a viable alternative to driving.

We will be assembling and installing the benches started during the 6/06 build.
