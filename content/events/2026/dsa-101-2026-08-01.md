Are you tired of a system that is rigged against the Working Class? Have you had enough frustration with the status quo, and want to do something about it?

At DSA, we believe a better world is possible. Join us to learn about the movement we are building, and how to create power for the only majority in this country, the Working Class!

What is Democratic Socialism? How does DSA work to achieve it? Which projects does Silicon Valley DSA work on? At our DSA 101 event, we will answer these questions and many others. This event is open to the public, so we encourage non-members come hear why we joined, and why we stuck around. Discover how we are helping the community, and how you can get involved to build a better world.

The event will be held at **SEIU 521 Union Hall** on **Saturday, August 1st** from **12:00 to 1:00 PM**.

Please **[RSVP HERE](https://actionnetwork.org/events/dsa-101-142?source=direct_link&)**.

Join us after the DSA 101 for our monthly Chapter Meeting, which starts at 1:00PM. You can talk to members, get more questions answered, and see a meeting in action.

To take part in Chapter decisions as a voting member, please sign up with National DSA at [https://dsausa.org/join](https://dsausa.org/join).
