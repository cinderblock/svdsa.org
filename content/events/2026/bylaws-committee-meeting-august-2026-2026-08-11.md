The Bylaws Committee is continuing our deliberate overhaul of the structure of SV-DSA in response to the Democracy Commission reforms passed by the 2025 National Convention. We meet monthly on the second Tuesday of each month to discuss our draft amendments and plan for how to introduce them to membership. Come join if you want to get involved in the nitty-gritty of helping refine and improve our democratic structure and operations!
