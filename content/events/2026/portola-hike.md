<p>Want to get some exercise and touch grass? Grab your hiking shoes and come out for a hike with your comrades! We will be hiking the Windy Hill Loop at the Windy Hill Open Space Preserve in the Portola Valley on Sunday, 5/31. The main trail is a 6.5 mile loop with about 1,400 feet of elevation gain, and is expected to take around three hours to complete. We also have shorter options with less elevation gain if you want to come but would prefer something less strenuous! We’ll be meeting at 9:00am and beginning the Windy Hill Loop at 9:15am. <br aria-hidden="true" />Please get in touch if you are interested in carpooling. Hope to see you there!</p>
<p>Trail Options and Alltrails Links: <br aria-hidden="true" /><a href="https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/windy-hill-loop">Windy Hill Loop</a> – 6.5 miles, 1,400 ft. elevation gain<br aria-hidden="true" /><a href="https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/toyon-trail-arroyo-trail-and-valley-oak-trail-loop">Toyon/Arroyo/Valley Oak Loop</a> – 4.1 miles, 600 ft. elevation gain<br aria-hidden="true" /><a href="https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/toyon-trail-and-coal-mine-ridge-loop">Toyon/Coal Mine Ridge Loop</a> – 3.3 miles, 511 ft. elevation gain</p>
<p>Trailhead Address: 4900 Alpine Rd, Portola Valley, CA 94028 <br aria-hidden="true" />Parking: Alpine Rd &amp; Willowbrook Dr, Portola Valley, CA 94028</p>
