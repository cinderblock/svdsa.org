Silicon Valley DSA holds quarterly leadership retreats so we can take a big picture view of our ongoing organizing work. We also work to develop member leaders and strategize about building coalitions with other local groups aligned with our goals and values. The retreat is open to all member leaders, and any members who are interested in stepping up into a larger role in the organization are encouraged to attend. We will hold a retreat at the SEIU 521 Union Hall. Come join us to help set the direction of the organization and work out strategies to empower our members to develop as leaders in SV DSA and the larger community.
