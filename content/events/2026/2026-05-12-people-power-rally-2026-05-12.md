On <b>Tuesday May 12</b>, workers from the City of San José will be rallying <b>at City Hall from 12:00pm - 1:00pm</b> to demand a fair contract that puts workers over billionaires, establishes guardrails on AI, and invests in the city staff who provide essential public services. We have been negotiating for over 2 months, but the City has not moved much from their opening positions. Please come out and show your support for city workers if you are available. Hope to see you there!
