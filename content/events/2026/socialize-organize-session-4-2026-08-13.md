Ever wondered how to form a union at work? Want to organize your workplace but don't know where to start?

Join us for an upcoming Fundamentals of Workplace Organizing training to hear from organizers and people like you who want to organize their workplaces! In small discussion groups with participants across Silicon Valley, you'll learn how to inoculate your coworkers from the bosses' threats and messaging when they carry out those threats. Critical to any organizing effort is preparing to resist the bosses' anti-union and union busting campaign that will inevitably occur.

Following the training, all attendees are invited to join us for a social at Pizza My Heart until 10pm! Need a ride there? Ask one of organizers!
