<p>Ever wondered how to form a union at work? Want to organize your workplace but don’t know where to start?</p>
<p>Join us for an upcoming Fundamentals of Workplace Organizing training to hear from organizers and people like you who want to organize their workplaces! In small discussion groups with participants across Silicon Valley, you&#8217;ll learn how to have an organizing conversation — the most basic building block of all organizing. Critical to any organizing effort is a conversation that can move people to take action!</p>
<p>Following the training, all attendees are invited to join us for a social at Peanuts Deluxe Cafe until 10pm! Need a ride there? Ask one of organizers!</p>
