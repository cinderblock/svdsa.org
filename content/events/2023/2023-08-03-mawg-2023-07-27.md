Join the mutual aid working group to help support community-based efforts in the South Bay! No experience is required and everyone has something to contribute! We will discuss:

*   supporting South Bay Mutual Aid (SBMA) as one of their coalition members
    *   sign up to volunteer with SBMA here: [https://southbay.aidnetwork.org](https://southbay.aidnetwork.org)
    *   donate to SBMA here by selecting the "Mutual Aid Fund": [https://siliconvalleydsa.org/donate/](https://siliconvalleydsa.org/donate/)
*   our current projects such as the really really free store, clothing drive, unhoused support, the garden box, and much more!
*   review current volunteer opportunities that can be done either in public with minimal contact (for example grocery delivery or flyering) or that can be done from home (for example coordinating aid, helping run meetings, helping research what supplies we should order)
